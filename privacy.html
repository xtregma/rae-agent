Rae Agent privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

What the app accesses

Rae Agent connects to the Gmail account of its single user, with that user's consent, using Google's Gmail API. It reads messages and labels, changes labels to organize mail, and sends email only to addresses the account owner has approved.

How the data is used

Email content is used only to provide the assistant's service to the account owner: summaries, search, organizing and replies the owner asks for. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is kept

The access token is stored only on the assistant's dedicated computer, readable by the assistant's own system account. Mail is read on demand and is not copied to any other service, except that the text being worked on is sent to the AI model provider that runs the assistant, under terms that exclude using it for training.

Deleting or revoking

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app never deletes email.
