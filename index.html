Rae Agent

Rae Agent is a private assistant used by one person at GMA Consulting. With that person's permission it connects to their own Gmail account so their assistant can read and search their mail, organize it with labels, and send email only to a short list of addresses the account owner has approved.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It never deletes email and never moves email to Trash or Spam.
